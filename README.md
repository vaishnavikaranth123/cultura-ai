# Cultura AI

## Human Creativity + Culture + Technology

Cultura AI is a creator-first digital platform designed to amplify human
creativity rather than replace artists.

The platform supports:

- Artists
- Musicians
- Writers
- Traditional artists
- Craftspeople
- Cultural creators
- Communities

## Core features

### Creator discovery

Users can discover independent creators by:

- Creator type
- Region
- Language
- Tradition
- Creative work

### Creator profiles

Every creator can create a digital profile containing:

- Name
- Bio
- Region
- Language
- Tradition
- Creative identity
- Social link

### Creative work

Creators can publish:

- Artwork
- Folk art
- Writing
- Craft
- Music descriptions
- Cultural work

### Creator-controlled stories

Creators provide their own description and cultural context.

Gemini helps transform that information into a public-facing story.

The creator remains the author.

### Accessibility

Cultura AI generates accessibility descriptions for creative works.

Browser text-to-speech is also available.

### Translation

Creator stories can be translated into multiple languages.

### AR / VR / Games

Gemini can generate concepts for:

- Augmented Reality
- Virtual Reality
- Educational games

The concepts are based on the creator's actual work.

### Preservation

The system can generate a future research and preservation plan.

Possible preservation areas:

- oral history
- cultural knowledge
- traditional techniques
- photographs
- videos
- metadata
- community participation
- future research
